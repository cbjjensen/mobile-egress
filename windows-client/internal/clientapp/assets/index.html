Phone not connecting?

If your phone already shows Stop, leave sharing running. Make sure this computer is enabled in the phone app and cellular data is available.

  • Check the public address and public port. A VPN or CGNAT can make a suggested address unreachable.
  • Check this computer’s firewall and its listener port.
  • Check router forwarding or the hosted server’s firewall, security group and internet route.

Check that Inevitable activation is approved and the gateway is connected.

For a cellular connection check, try turning off Wi-Fi on the phone. On iPhone, keep-awake prevents automatic locking while sharing; it does not enable background sharing.

This computer’s address

Your phone connects to this computer over cellular. Enter a public hostname or IP address that can accept incoming connections.

The ipify lookup service sees your outgoing public IP. A suggested address is not yet verified: VPNs, NAT and CGNAT may make it unsuitable for incoming connections. You can always enter your own address.

Advanced: listener and public port

Both ports default to 8443. Forward the public port to the local listener port if they differ. Keep the local HTTP and SOCKS proxy ports 1080 and 1081 private.

Changing an existing endpoint may interrupt connections. Pairing and proxy credentials are retained; the paired phone may need a connection update.

Activate Inevitable

Activate this Client in your browser, then pair your phone locally. Your phone does not need an Inevitable login. The Client and phone connect outbound; application traffic exits through cellular.

Keep this app open while approving. Activation can resume after restarting the Client service. Only a live authenticated phone connection verifies setup.
