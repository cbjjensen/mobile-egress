## Reference images and variant prompts

### 1A — Cellular · Crisp

Reference: `../../assets/branding/symbol-concepts-2026-10-06/01-cellular.png`.

Refine the cellular-signal idea into a precise restrained emblem: three equal-width solid WHITE rising bars, evenly spaced, with flat horizontal tops and aligned bottom baseline. Moderate corner softness only. Make the outer white upper arc continuous and perfectly smooth, removing the awkward broken top and right-angle notch from the original. Leave an even black clearance between all bar tops and the outer arc. The circle's sides end cleanly above the purple and red stones. This version should look meticulously balanced and clean.

### 1B — Cellular · Rounded

Reference: `../../assets/branding/symbol-concepts-2026-10-06/01-cellular.png`.

Create a friendlier, very simple cellular emblem using three thick ascending vertical WHITE pill-shaped bars with fully rounded ends. Remove the separate white enclosing circle entirely. The tall trio and the lower curved stone arc provide the silhouette together. Keep the bars centered above the stones, substantial, with large clear black gaps. Keep ample breathing room between the bars and gems. This version is an open, softer interpretation: just three rounded bars plus the six stones.

### 1C — Cellular · Sculpted

Reference: `../../assets/branding/symbol-concepts-2026-10-06/01-cellular.png`.

Create a bold custom cellular emblem with FOUR ascending white signal columns cut from a single near-circular white shape. The column tops climb diagonally from lower-left to upper-right; the tallest column's outer edge becomes the upper-right circle contour. Use angular beveled tips and carefully proportioned black channels separating columns, with no separate thin circle ring around them. The large white silhouettes feel sculpted and distinctive, a cohesive circular mark like the original ZFNF letter geometry, while remaining clearly four rising signal bars. Stones still complete the bottom arc.

### 4A — Broadcast · Balanced

Reference: `../../assets/branding/symbol-concepts-2026-10-06/04-broadcast.png`.

Refine the original radio-broadcast mark with a smaller central transmitter dot, a shorter narrow stem, and two symmetrical radiating arcs on either side. Use consistent moderately thick white strokes, broad negative space, and carefully matched arc endpoints. Reduce the heavy keyhole-like center of the original: the center should be a compact radio beacon rather than a person or lock. Keep the outermost wave arcs as the sides of the circle, framing the six stones below. Crisp, balanced and recognizable.

### 4B — Broadcast · Beacon

Reference: `../../assets/branding/symbol-concepts-2026-10-06/04-broadcast.png`.

Make a minimal stemless broadcast beacon: one centered solid WHITE circular dot with two thick smooth concentric WHITE curved brackets on each side. NO vertical stem. Use gracefully rounded arc ends, generous even black gaps and bilateral symmetry. The four bracket arcs radiate outward from the dot and form most of a circle, open at top and bottom. Keep the white symbol compact so the six stones below remain a clear lower arc. This is the simplest, friendliest radio signal version.

### 4C — Broadcast · Faceted

Reference: `../../assets/branding/symbol-concepts-2026-10-06/04-broadcast.png`.

Create an angular broadcast emblem with one small WHITE solid diamond at the center, flanked by two expanding WHITE chevron-shaped radio-wave bands on EACH side. The bands bend around the center like a geometric signal, with faceted corners and softened outer circular contours. No vertical stem. Use consistent thick strokes and clean black separations. It should look like a bold, sculpted cellular broadcast symbol whose geometry subtly echoes the six gemstones below, not wings, not an eye, and not a Wi-Fi fan. Keep the overall composition circular.
