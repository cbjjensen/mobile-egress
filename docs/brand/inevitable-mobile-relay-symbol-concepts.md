## Shared generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one square mobile app icon concept for Inevitable Mobile Relay.
Reference image: the approved visual direction. Preserve its black background, strong circular overall silhouette, crisp bold white geometry, and especially the SIX faceted colored stones along the bottom arc.
The user explicitly wants SYMBOLS, NOT LETTERS. Completely remove the IMR letterforms and replace them with the symbol described below. No letters or numbers anywhere.
The six stones must remain clearly present, exactly six, left-to-right purple, blue, green, yellow, orange, red. Preserve the reference's faceted shape language, broad tonal planes, black separation and curved lower arc. The stones occupy about the lower quarter of the circular emblem. They are a restrained integral brand element.
Design the upper white symbol and lower stones to read together as one cohesive circular badge. Use simple thick white forms and crisp negative space inspired by the reference's geometric style. Refine optical balance. Make the symbol easily readable at small app-icon sizes.
Centered emblem occupies the central 74 percent of an opaque square black canvas. Even outer padding. Black extends to every edge, no baked-in rounded tile corners.
Style: precise premium near-flat geometric logo, restrained, clean, no illustration clutter. Only the stones have subtle facet shading; white stays flat white.
Constraints: no IMR, no MR, no ZFNF, no text, no caption, no number, no literal phone, no extra colored stones, no mockup, no metallic finish, no glow, no particles or watermark. Deliver ONE finished icon, not a sheet.
SPECIFIC SYMBOL:
```

## Per-concept prompt additions

### 1. Cellular

Three bold ascending cellular signal bars, cut to follow the circular badge contour. Strong straightforward mobile-data recognition. Use vertical solid bars with elegant squared ends and a generous black gap between each. A thick white upper/side circular arc may join the bars to form one cohesive mark.

### 2. Exchange

Two thick opposing horizontal transfer arrows, one pointing right above one pointing left. Their outer tails curve with the circular badge edge, creating a tightly integrated bidirectional relay emblem. Clearly visible arrowheads, very few strokes.

### 3. Linked

Two bold interlocking rounded-square chain links on a rising diagonal. Resolve the over-under crossing cleanly with black cuts. The two white links fill the upper circular area as one strong paired-device symbol. No infinity-shaped ribbon.

### 4. Broadcast

A central white transmitter dot with two thick symmetrical pairs of radiating arcs on both sides, like cellular radio coverage. A compact vertical center stem supports the dot. Generous negative space, elegantly integrated into a circular silhouette. It must be a bold abstract broadcasting mark, not a detailed antenna illustration or a Wi-Fi fan.

### 5. Spark

One strong angular lightning bolt slashing downward through a near-circular white disk. Design with black negative-space cuts so the bolt reads as a crisp, memorable white silhouette within the circular shape. A bold simple energy/data-transfer mark, balanced and legible.

### 6. Bridge

A single bold white bridge arch connecting two substantial upright endpoints. The curved span follows the upper circle and the two endpoint pillars curve with its side edges. Minimal abstract connectivity emblem with clean negative-space opening beneath the bridge, no architectural details or road illustration.

### 7. Handoff

Two white rounded diamond-shaped endpoint nodes, one upper-left and one lower-right, joined by a thick clean stepped connector. Use black negative-space cuts and contour shaping to make a cohesive compact circular handoff symbol. Just two nodes and one connection, not a network diagram.

### 8. Pulse

A single thick smooth white waveform travelling across a circular badge from left to right, one rising crest and one trough. The waveform joins the curved outside edge to make a strong sculptural emblem. Convey flowing data, not a medical ECG: smooth broad curves, no sharp heartbeat spikes.

### 9. Portal

A bold white open circular gateway or portal with two offset crescent-shaped blades sweeping toward a clean black center. Asymmetric but balanced; a subtle offset gap makes it feel like data passing through a gateway. Two large white shapes only, no arrows, no letters, no tiny orbital objects.

### 10. Globe

A simplified bold white globe formed from a thick outer upper/side circle, one central curved meridian and one horizontal equator. Minimal broad strokes and large clean black openings. The six colored stones replace the bottom edge of the globe. Convey mobile access to the Internet.
