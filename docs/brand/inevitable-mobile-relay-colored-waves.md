```text
Retain the six brand color families purple, blue, green, yellow, orange, red, but ONLY as color on radio waves. There must be NO gems, NO gemstones, NO colored lower necklace/arc, NO detached colored blocks or decorative objects. The bottom must be clean empty black beyond the radio symbol. Center the whole new composition after removing the stones.

Square opaque true-black canvas, equal generous safe margins with all artwork inside central 74 percent. Flat white central transmitter detail as specified below.
No letters, initials, words, numbers, captions, watermark, realistic objects, phone, computer, mockup, lens flare, neon glow, glitter, chrome or background texture.
Deliver ONE standalone finished icon, not a concept sheet.
VARIATION:
```

## Variant prompts

### W1 — Solid bands

Reference: `../../assets/branding/symbol-concepts-2026-10-06/04-broadcast.png`.

Use a white circular transmitter dot with a short white stem, centered, clearly a radio beacon. Surround it with THREE thick concentric radio arcs on EACH side (six separate arcs total), evenly spaced and with subtly rounded ends. Assign one flat brand color to each entire arc: left outer purple, left middle royal blue, left inner emerald green; right inner yellow, right middle orange, right outer red. Thus exactly six solid-colored wave bands carry all six brand colors. Preserve bilateral geometry but not bilateral colors. Rebalance the full radio emblem so it fills a compact circle with equal top and bottom opening. Keep all wave bands broad, bold and simple. No gradient, no facet shading, no objects below the beacon.

### W2 — Segmented waves

Reference: `../../assets/branding/signal-refinements-2026-10-06/4b-broadcast-beacon.png`.

Create a stemless white central dot with TWO thick smooth radio brackets on each side, four arcs total, like the reference. Use the six brand colors as crisp flat color SECTIONS along the wave arcs: each left arc has purple at top, royal blue at middle, emerald green at bottom; each right arc has red at top, orange at middle, yellow at bottom. Colors meet at very thin clean black radial seams, forming regular curved-band sections, NOT gems or polygons. Maintain perfect circular curvature, rounded outer arc endpoints and equal negative-space spacing. Remove all content below the waves and recenter the now-complete symbol. The colored arc segments provide a refined recognizable brand identity.

### W3 — Gradient waves

Reference: `../../assets/branding/signal-refinements-2026-10-06/4b-broadcast-beacon.png`.

Create a clean minimal stemless WHITE central dot and two concentric smoothly curved radio-wave brackets on each side, four brackets total. All four wave bands carry a smooth rich SIX-COLOR spectral gradient, purple into royal blue into emerald green into yellow into orange into ruby red, flowing continuously from the left side to the right side of the emblem. The left side is predominantly purple and blue, center-facing ends introduce green and yellow, right side is orange and red. Smooth premium color interpolation within the arc shapes, no glow, no blur. Thick arcs with fully rounded ends, generous gaps, crisp silhouette, even opening above and below. Strong app icon rather than a colorful illustration. Center dot remains pure white.

### W4 — Angular waves

Reference: `../../assets/branding/symbol-concepts-2026-10-06/04-broadcast.png`.

Create a small WHITE circular transmitter dot with a short straight white stem. Around it use TWO expanding angular radio-wave bands on each side, four bands total. Curve the broad outer silhouette into a circle, but give the inner band contours subtle chamfered bends echoing faceted stone geometry. Put the six brand colors directly into the broad wave bands: left waves transition through distinct purple, blue and emerald sections; right waves through yellow, orange and ruby red sections. Add only one restrained tonal plane per color section to evoke faceting, while each band remains a continuous radio wave. No gem-shaped pieces, no detached decorations, no bottom arc. It should look like colored radio waves cut from geometric material. Crisp small-icon readability and balanced optical spacing.
